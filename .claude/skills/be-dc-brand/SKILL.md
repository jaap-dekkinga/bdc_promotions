# Be DC brand rules

Source of truth: `what-is-be-dc.md` in the Be DC project folder. Owner: Jaap Dekkinga. Update this skill only with his sign-off.

## The mark
- Written **B★DC** (B, five-pointed star, DC). Said "Be DC". Never "BDC" or "B-DC" in customer copy.
- Meaning, for copy: the **B** comes from the Constitution's handwriting; **DC** is in Times New Roman, the typeface of US law, filled with flag stripes; the **★** is a placeholder for any message ("Be ___").
- Use only the existing logo files in `assets/img/` (`logo-color.png`, `logo-black.png`, `logo-white.png`). Don't redraw, recolor, stretch or add effects.
- Trademark is pending (serial 99376168). Use ™ at most, never ®.

Headlines: Big Shoulders Display 700–900. Body: Inter 400–700. Labels, prices, data: IBM Plex Mono.
No other brand colors. Neutrals (greys) only for text and borders.

## Voice
- Proud, short, a bit playful. Iconic city brand, like I ♥ NY or I amsterdam, never touristy kitsch.
- Signature lines: "Wear it, say it." / "You can ♥ NY, but everybody wants to Be DC."
- Every product and campaign gets its own **Be ___** phrase: one verb or adjective, lowercase after "Be" (Be seen, Be warm, Be awake). Check that it's not already used by another product.
- Don't claim the city, DC.gov, or any partner endorses Be DC unless there is a signed agreement. Partner phrases (Be challenged = Marine Corps Marathon, etc.) are proposals, not endorsements.

## DC facts
- One fact per product or post, one sentence, checkable.
- Prefer stable history facts (L'Enfant plan 1791, Washington Monument 555 ft, Height Act). Before using a number that changes (population, visitors, rankings), look it up and cite the year.
- Statistics from the 2010 research must be framed as "when the project started in 2010…", never as current.
